This is a free site for people who are new to trading and investing. The goal is simple: explain how markets work in plain words, and help you avoid the mistakes that cost beginners the most.

## What you'll find here

- **A course** in short modules: how markets work, survive first, read the market, build a system, the trader's mind, and going further. Three stages, in order: foundations, skills, then mind and the real world.
- **Articles** that go deeper on single ideas.
- **A glossary** of common terms, explained without jargon.

## What you won't find here

- No tips, signals or "what to buy" lists.
- No price predictions or price targets.
- No hype. Markets are hard, and we'll say so.

## Examples and sources

Examples marked *hypothetical* are made up to show the arithmetic clearly. Real-world examples name their source and date.

## Not financial advice

Everything here is for education only. It is not financial advice, and it doesn't consider your personal situation. Trading carries risk, including the loss of the money you put in. If you need advice, talk to a licensed professional.
