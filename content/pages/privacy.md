This is a free education site. You can read the home page, articles, glossary and the first module without an account. To read the rest of the course you need a free account, so we can save your progress.

## What we store

When you create an account we store:

- **Your email address**, so you can sign in and so the account is yours.
- **Your course progress**: which lessons you marked complete and whether your answers to the lesson quick questions were right.
- **When** you created the account, last signed in and last saved progress.
- **Whether you want emails about new lessons.** This is off unless you tick the box, and you can turn it off any time on your account page.

To sign you in we also keep the one-time code we email you (scrambled, never in plain text) for up to a day.

We don't store passwords, names, payment details, or anything about your trading. We don't use analytics, ads or tracking cookies, and we don't sell or share your data.

## Cookies

When you sign in we set two small cookies: one that keeps you signed in (for 30 days), and one that only tells the page you're signed in so it can show "Your account". They are needed for the account to work, so we don't ask for consent for them. Your theme choice and, before you sign in, your progress are saved in your own browser.

## Why we can use your data

We use your email and progress to provide the account you asked for. We send emails about new lessons only if you opted in, and you can withdraw that at any time.

## Who handles it for us

- **Vercel** hosts the site.
- **Neon** hosts the database with your email and progress.
- **Resend** sends the sign-in email.

These companies process data for us under their own data protection terms. Some of them may process data outside the EU.

## Your rights

On your account page you can **download all your data** (a JSON file) and **delete your account**. Deleting removes your email and progress from our database straight away.

You can also ask us to access, correct, delete or limit the use of your data, or object to how we use it. If you're not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es).

## How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. Sign-in codes are removed within a day.
