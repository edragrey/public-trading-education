# What a market is, step by step

One small idea per screen. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## A meeting place

A **market** is anywhere buyers and sellers can find each other and agree on a price.

## Two things make it work

1. **Something to trade.** A share, a coin, a barrel of oil, a currency.
2. **A way to match people.** Usually an exchange or a broker that keeps a list of who wants to buy and sell.

## Owning, or betting on the price

- **Spot:** you buy the share or coin and **own** it.
- **Derivatives:** you hold a **contract** about the price and never own the thing. They often allow leverage, which makes gains and losses bigger.

Same chart, very different risks.

## Key idea

A market is a meeting place for buyers and sellers. Spot means owning the thing, derivatives mean a contract about its price, and the second is riskier. For the full picture, read the lesson.
