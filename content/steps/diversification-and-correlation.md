# Diversification and correlation, step by step

One small idea per screen. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Two words

**Diversification:** not putting all your money in one thing.

**Correlation:** how much two things move together. **High** means they usually rise and fall together. **Low** means they move more independently.

## The trap

> **Hypothetical example.** You hold five small coins and risk 1% on each, so 5% in total. A market-wide sell-off hits and all five fall to their stops together.

You lose about **5%**, not 1%. That's one bet repeated five times.

## Where hidden concentration hides

- **Same market:** most coins fall together in a big move.
- **Same theme:** coins from one hot narrative are one idea in disguise.
- **Same direction:** five longs when the market drops.
- **Leverage:** it amplifies every one of them.

<!-- step -->

## How to limit it

1. **Count open risk, not trades.** Many traders cap the total, say 3 to 5% of the account.
2. **Group by theme.**
3. **Stress test:** "If everything fell on the same day, how much would I lose?"
4. **Keep some cash.** Not being invested is a position too.

**Key idea:** Several positions that move together are one big bet. Count total open risk, not the number of trades.
