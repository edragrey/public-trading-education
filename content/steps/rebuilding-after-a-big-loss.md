# Rebuilding after a big loss, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Process first, account second

After a big loss or a long break, rebuild your **process** first. Your account follows.

Experience helps you notice things, but it is not an edge.

<!-- step -->

## A ladder back

> **Hypothetical.**
> 1. Paper trade for weeks.
> 2. A quarter of your old size.
> 3. Half size after 20 rule-following trades.
> 4. Normal size after another stretch.

Break a rule? Step down one rung.

## Don't chase the number

Down 50% needs +100% to recover. Trying to do it fast means big risks.

The slow way is usually the only reliable one.

**Key idea:** rebuild slowly, with smaller size and paper trading first.
