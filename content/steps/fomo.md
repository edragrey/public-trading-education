# FOMO, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## You know this feeling

A coin you were watching is up **20%**. Then **35%**. Your feed is full of people celebrating.

Something says: **now, before it's too late.**

<!-- step -->

## It has a name

**FOMO**: the fear of missing out.

Almost every trader feels it. The feeling is normal. The trouble is what it pushes you to do.

<!-- step -->

## What FOMO makes people do

- Buy **after** a big move, near a short-term high.
- Skip the plan: no setup, no clear stop.
- Trade **too big**, to make up for "missed" gains.

<!-- step -->

## A hypothetical chase

A coin runs from **$1.00** to **$1.60** in two days.

A beginner buys at **$1.55**, no stop. Two days later it's back at **$1.20**.

That's **−23%**, on a trade made because of a feeling.

<!-- step -->

## Habit 1: name it

When the urge hits, say it: **"This is FOMO."**

Naming a feeling makes it easier to watch, instead of obey.

<!-- step -->

## Habit 2: run the checklist anyway

If it's a real trade, it passes **all six gates**.

If the price has already run, you often can't place a sensible stop or get a decent reward-to-risk. The checklist gives you a calm **no**.

<!-- step -->

<!-- step -->

## Habit 3: there's always another trade

Markets open every day. Missing one move costs nothing.

**Chasing** it can cost a lot.

<!-- step -->

## You've got it

**FOMO is a feeling, not a signal. Name it, run your checklist, and remember that missing a move costs nothing.**
