# Percentages, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **4 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Divide by where you started

**Percent change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100.**

> **Hypothetical.** $50 to $60 is $10 up. $10 ÷ $50 = **+20%**.

<!-- step -->

## Quick check

A price goes from $40 to $50. What is the percent change?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">+10%</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">+25%</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">+20%</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>**+25%.** $10 ÷ $40 = 0.25.</p>
</div>

<!-- step -->

## Going back is not the same

$60 back to $50 is −$10, but now you divide by $60: **−16.7%**.

Same dollars, different percent, because the starting point moved.

<!-- step -->

## Losses need bigger gains

| Lose | Need to get back |
| --- | --- |
| 10% | 11% |
| 25% | 33% |
| 50% | 100% |

That is why small losses matter so much.

<!-- step -->

## Quick check

You lose 50% of your money. What gain gets you back to even?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="c">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">+50%</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">+75%</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">+100%</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>**+100%.** $100 → $50 needs to double to be $100 again.</p>
</div>

**Key idea:** percent change always divides by where you started, so losses are harder to undo than they look.
