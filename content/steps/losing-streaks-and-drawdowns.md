# Losing streaks and drawdowns, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **4 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Streaks are normal

Even a good system has runs of losses. A 50% win rate can easily produce six or seven losses in a row.

Plan for it before it happens.

<!-- step -->

## Drawdown

A **drawdown** is the fall from your account's highest point.

If it went from $10,000 to $8,000, that's a **20% drawdown**.

<!-- step -->

## Recovery is not symmetrical

> **Hypothetical.** Lose 50%, from $1,000 to $500. To get back to $1,000 you need to **double**: a 100% gain.

The deeper the hole, the harder the climb.

<!-- step -->

## Rules written in advance

- **Cut size** after a drawdown of, say, 10%.
- **Pause** after a set number of losses.
- **Hard floor:** stop real trading at a set level and return to paper.

<!-- step -->

## Quick check

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">

## You've got it

**Streaks are normal and recovery is harder than the fall. Keep risk small and write your rules in advance.**
