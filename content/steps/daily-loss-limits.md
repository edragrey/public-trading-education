# Daily loss limits, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

> **Hypothetical examples.** The account and trades are made up, but the arithmetic is exact.

<!-- step -->

## Willpower runs out

After a few losses you're tired, frustrated, and desperate to turn the day around.

That's exactly when willpower is **weakest**.

<!-- step -->

## The fix: a rule set while calm

**Daily loss limit:** the most you allow yourself to lose in one day.

Reach it, and you **stop until tomorrow**. No exceptions.

<!-- step -->

## Without a limit

Account **$2,000**. Plan: risk 1% ($20).

−$20, then a revenge trade −$20, then double size −$40, then no stop −$150.

**−$230** in one afternoon. Over 11%.

<!-- step -->

## With a 3% limit

Same account. Limit: **3%**, so **$60**.

−$20, −$20, −$20. **Limit reached. Stop.**

Worst possible day: **about −3%**.

## A simple rule of thumb

**Daily limit = 2 or 3 losing trades.**

**Weekly limit = about twice the daily.**

Write both on your trade plan.

<!-- step -->

## Count what's still open

If a stop on a trade you're **still in** would take you past the limit, don't open another one.

<!-- step -->

## When you hit it, leave

Close the app. Don't "just watch". Watching leads to trading.

Hitting the limit isn't failure. It's the moment the rule **saved** you.

## You've got it

**Decide how much you can lose in a day before the day starts. When you reach it, stop.**
