# Hype cycles, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

> **No predictions here.** This explains a common pattern. It can't tell you where any real coin is in its cycle.

<!-- step -->

## Markets love a story

"AI will change everything." "This is the next big gaming coin."

A **narrative** is a story that ties a group of coins or stocks together. Money tends to rush into the hottest one, then out again.

## Stage 1: quiet start

A few people are building or talking about it.

Prices barely move. **Hardly anyone notices.**

<!-- step -->

## Stage 2: discovery

The main coins start rising. More people notice.

The first articles and posts appear.

<!-- step -->

## Stage 3: excitement

It's everywhere. Big daily moves.

**Copycat tokens** launch to ride the story, often with little behind them.

<!-- step -->

## Stage 4: peak attention

Friends and family ask about it. Your feed is full of it.

Volume is huge.

<!-- step -->

## Stage 5: fade

New buyers dry up. Early holders sell. Prices fall, often fast.

Attention moves on to the **next** story.

## The uncomfortable part

By the time a story is all over your feed, the people posting loudest have often **already bought**.

You and they can be on very different points of the same cycle.

## Before you buy into a story

Ask four things:

1. Which stage does this **look** like?
2. Is there real **volume and liquidity**?
3. How much **supply** is still to be released?
4. Can I write down **why** I'm buying, beyond "everyone's talking about it"?

<!-- step -->

## You've got it

**Stories pull money in and push it out again. The loudest moment is rarely the earliest one, so trade the plan, not the hype.**
