# Your first paper trade, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **5 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## The goal

Practise one full trade with **no real money**.

You'll plan it, size it, place it, protect it and review it. The result doesn't matter. The habit does.

<!-- step -->

## Step 1: write the plan

Five short lines, before you click anything:

- The asset
- Why you're taking it
- Your entry
- Your exit if you're wrong (the stop)
- Your exit if you're right

<!-- step -->

## Step 2: size it from the stop

> **Hypothetical.** Pretend risk: $100. Entry $50. Stop $48.

The stop is $2 away. **$100 ÷ $2 = 50 coins.**

Size comes from the stop, not from how confident you feel.

<!-- step -->

## Quick check

Pretend risk $60. Entry $30, stop $29. How many units?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">

<button type="button" data-opt="b">30 units</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden><strong>60 units.</strong> The stop is $1 away, and $60 ÷ $1 = 60.</p>
</div>

<!-- step -->

## Step 3: place it and protect it

Place your entry with the order type you chose.

When it fills, **place the stop straight away**. A position with no stop isn't a plan.

<!-- step -->

## Step 4: leave it alone

Don't move your stop farther away because you hope. Don't add size to "fix" a loss.

Let it reach the stop, the target or your written reason to leave.

<!-- step -->

## Step 5: review in two lines

1. What happened?
2. Did I follow my plan?

A loss that followed the plan is a good trade. A win that broke the plan is a warning.

<!-- step -->

## Quick check

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">Nothing, a win is a win</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">Note it as a rule break and fix the habit</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden><strong>Fix the habit.</strong> Luck covers a bad habit until it doesn't.</p>
</div>

<!-- step -->

## Done

You've done one full trade loop. Do a few more, keep the reviews, and the habit sticks.

**Key idea:** Plan first, size from your stop, protect the trade, then review.
