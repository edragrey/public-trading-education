# How to use this course, step by step

One idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Take one lesson at a time

The course is designed to be read in order. Each module gives you a piece of the process, and later modules use it again.

You do not need to finish everything in one sitting.

<!-- step -->

## A good rhythm

1. Read one lesson.
2. Look up unfamiliar words in the glossary.
3. Answer the questions without looking first.
4. Write one idea in your own words.
5. Practise it on a chart or paper trade.

## Do not skip the boring parts

Chart patterns and the latest narrative may be exciting. But deciding your risk, placing an order correctly, recording what happened and reviewing your behaviour are the skills that protect you.

The course follows that order deliberately: mechanics before tactics, survival before size, and a written process before confidence.

<!-- step -->

## What counts as progress

Progress is not finding a trade that went up. It is being able to explain:

- what you expected;
- what could have proved you wrong;
- how much you were willing to lose;
- whether you followed your plan; and
- what you would change next time.

<!-- step -->

## Keep the cost small

You can practise all of this before putting money at risk. When you eventually study a live trade, keep the amount small enough that being wrong does not end your learning.

**Key idea:** Learn the process first. Keep the cost of being wrong small while you are learning.
