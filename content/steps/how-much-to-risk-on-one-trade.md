# How much to risk, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Risk per trade

**Risk** is how much you'd lose if the trade hits your stop.

A common rule: risk about **1%** of your account on any one trade.

<!-- step -->

## Why 1%

Losing streaks happen. With small risk they're survivable.

> **Hypothetical.** Ten losses in a row at 1% leaves you about 90% of your account. At 10%, about 35%.

<!-- step -->

## Risk is not position size

Risk is the loss at your stop. Position size is how many units you buy.

A $20 risk can be a big position with a tight stop or a small one with a wide stop.

<!-- step -->

## Start smaller if you like

Beginners can use 0.25% or 0.5% while learning. There's no prize for risking more.

**Key idea:** Pick a small fixed risk per trade and size every trade from it.
