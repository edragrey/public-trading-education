# Staying safe, step by step

One small idea per screen. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Three words

**Scam:** any scheme designed to take your money by lying to you.

**Seed phrase:** the 12 or 24 words that control a crypto wallet. Whoever has it controls the money.

**Two-factor authentication (2FA):** a second check when you log in, like a code from an app on your phone.

## Common scams in crypto

- **Fake support** with a link that steals your login.
- **Rug pulls:** a token is hyped, then the creators sell everything.
- **Pump and dumps:** a group hypes a small coin, then sells to the people who rushed in.
- **Fake websites:** a copy of a real exchange with one letter changed.

<!-- step -->

## Your safety checklist

1. Turn on **2FA** with an app, not just SMS.
2. Use a **unique, strong password** in a password manager.
3. Type or bookmark website addresses yourself.
4. Keep your **seed phrase offline**, never in photos or chats.
5. Start with **amounts you can afford to lose**.

**Key idea:** Protect your accounts before you protect your trades.
