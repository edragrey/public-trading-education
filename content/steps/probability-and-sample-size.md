# Sample size, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Luck looks like skill

> **Hypothetical.** A trader with **no edge** (a pure 50/50) still wins 7 or more of 10 trades about **1 time in 6**.

A few trades prove almost nothing.

<!-- step -->

## Quick check

A trader wins 7 of 10. Does that prove skill?

<!-- step -->

## More trades, more truth

- 10 trades: almost nothing
- 30: a hint
- 100: starting to mean something
- 200+: a reasonable picture

And only if every trade followed the **same rules** and every loser was recorded.

<!-- step -->

## Quick check

You are on a 5-win streak. Should you double your size?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="c">

**Key idea:** small samples are mostly luck, so gather many honest trades before you trust a result.
