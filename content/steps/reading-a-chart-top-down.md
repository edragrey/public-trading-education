# Reading a chart top-down, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Big picture first

Start on a higher timeframe and zoom in:

1. Trend
2. Key areas
3. Where is price now?
4. Extras (volume, averages, RSI)
5. Timing on a lower timeframe
6. Written plan

<!-- step -->

## Confluence

Several separate clues pointing the same way are stronger than one. None is a guarantee.

When clues **disagree**, waiting is a fine answer.

## End with a plan

Entry, stop (sized with ATR), target and size. No plan, no trade.

**Key idea:** read in order, big picture first, and finish with a written plan.
