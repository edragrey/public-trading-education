# Risk and reward, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Two distances

For any trade there are two distances from your entry:

- **Risk:** how far to your stop.
- **Reward:** how far to your target.

<!-- step -->

## The ratio

**Reward-to-risk** compares them.

> **Hypothetical.** Entry $50, stop $48, target $56. Risk $2, reward $6. That's **3 to 1**.

<!-- step -->

Entry $20, stop $19, target $22. What's the reward-to-risk?

<!-- step -->

## It works with win rate

A higher ratio means you can win less often and still do well. A low ratio needs a high win rate.

A 50% win rate with 1 to 1 breaks even (before costs). With 2 to 1 it can be profitable.

<!-- step -->

## Quick check

Which pair is likely worse over time?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">70% win rate, but wins are half the size of losses</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">40% win rate, wins are twice the size of losses</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden><strong>The first.</strong> 0.7 × 0.5 - 0.3 = +0.05R, thin and easily erased by costs. The second is 0.4 × 2 - 0.6 = +0.2R.</p>
</div>

<!-- step -->

## Don't force it

Don't pick a target just to get a nice ratio. Targets should come from the chart, not hope.

**Key idea:** Reward-to-risk and win rate work together. Costs matter too.
