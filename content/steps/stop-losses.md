# Stop-losses, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **4 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## What a stop is

A **stop-loss** is a pre-set exit that closes a trade if price moves against you by a chosen amount.

It's your plan for being wrong, written before the trade.

<!-- step -->

## Where it goes

Not "wherever I can bear to lose".

Put it where **the idea is proven wrong**: for example just below a recent swing low (the bottom of a recent dip).

Then size the trade so that loss is about 1% of your account.

## Too tight, too wide

- **Too tight:** normal noise stops you out again and again.
- **Too wide:** a loss bigger than you meant.

Fix width with **smaller size**, not a hopeful stop.

<!-- step -->

## The one rule

**Never move a stop further away** to avoid a loss.

You may move it to reduce risk, for example to break-even.

## Gaps happen

In very fast moves price can jump past your stop. That's why **size** is your first protection.

**Key idea:** The stop is where you're wrong. Set it before you enter and never widen it.
