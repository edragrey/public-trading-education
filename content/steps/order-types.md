# Order types, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **4 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## What an order is

An **order** is your instruction to the market.

The **type** of order decides how and when it fills.

<!-- step -->

## Market order: now

"Buy (or sell) **right now** at the best price available."

- Fills straight away.
- You pay the spread, and a big order may get a worse price (slippage).

<!-- step -->

## Quick check

The best ask is $50.10. You place a market buy. About what do you pay?

<!-- step -->

## Limit order: my price

"Buy (or sell) **only at this price or better**."

- A buy limit sits **below** the price. A sell limit sits **above**.
- You control the price.
- It may **never fill**.

<!-- step -->

## Quick check

A coin is at $100. You want to buy only if it falls to $98. Which order?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">A buy limit at $98</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">A market buy</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden><strong>A buy limit at $98.</strong> It waits for your price. A market buy would fill at $100 now.</p>
</div>

<!-- step -->

## Stop order: a trigger

A **stop** waits until price reaches a trigger, then becomes an order.

A **stop-loss** is your safety exit. It limits a loss if you're wrong.

A stop-market fills fast but can slip. A stop-limit controls the price but may not fill.

<!-- step -->

## Quick check

Why can a stop-market fill worse than your stop price?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">Exchanges add a fee</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">In a fast move there may be no buyers at your price</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden><strong>Fast moves.</strong> Once triggered it's a market order, so it takes the next best price.</p>
</div>

<!-- step -->

## Which one when?

- In now, small slippage OK: **market**.
- A price in mind, can wait: **limit**.
- Safety exit if wrong: **stop-loss**.

**Key idea:** The order type decides how you trade. Pick it on purpose.
