# Timeframes, step by step

One small idea per screen. About **3 minutes**.

> **Hypothetical example.** One made-up coin, three ways.

<!-- step -->

## One word

**Timeframe:** how much time each candle covers. On a 1-hour chart each candle is an hour. On a daily chart, a day.

<!-- step -->

## Shorter means noisier

On a 1-minute chart, a lot of movement is just single orders bumping the price. Very short timeframes also mean more trades, more fees and faster, more emotional decisions.

<!-- step -->

## Match the timeframe to the trade

- Holding for **weeks:** daily and weekly charts.
- Holding for **days:** daily and 4-hour.
- Holding for **hours:** 1-hour and 15-minute.

Then **check one timeframe higher** than the one you trade. It shows the bigger picture your trade sits inside.

**Key idea:** A chart's story depends on its timeframe. Choose the one that matches how long you plan to hold.
