# Changing your rules, step by step

One small idea per screen, with quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## The urge

A rough patch arrives, and you want to change your system.

Sometimes that's right. Often it's the **worst** moment to do it.

<!-- step -->

## Mistake 1: changing too fast

Three losses, add a rule. Two more, change the stop. After a month you've tested five systems for a week each.

You've learned **nothing** about any of them.

<!-- step -->

## Mistake 2: never changing

Ignoring clear evidence over many trades because "the system is the system".

Rules should evolve. Just not every Tuesday.

<!-- step -->

## Question 1: did I follow the rules?

Check your journal. If the losses come from **broken** rules, the problem is behaviour, not the system.

<!-- step -->

## Question 2: is the sample big enough?

Under about **30 trades**, results are mostly chance.

Wait.

<!-- step -->

## Question 3: is the problem specific?

"It isn't working" is not specific.

"A third of my losses are bigger than −1R, all in the first hour after the US open" **is**. A specific problem suggests a specific fix.

## When you do change

- **One change at a time**, so you know what helped.
- **Write it down** with a date, as a new plan version.
- Give it **30 or more trades** before judging.
