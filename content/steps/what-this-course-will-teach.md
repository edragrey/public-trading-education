# What this course will teach you, step by step

One idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **4 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Trading is more than prediction

Trading can look like guessing whether a price will rise or fall.

That is part of it. But you also need to decide how much to risk, when your idea is wrong, how to place an order and what to do when the market surprises you.

<!-- step -->

## The course path

You will learn, in order:

- How markets work
- How to protect your money
- How to read a market
- How to write and test a trading process
- How emotions and probabilities affect results

## First: how markets work

You will learn what a market is, where trades happen, how buyers and sellers create a price, what orders do and how to read a basic chart.

You will also see what is different about stocks, commodities and crypto.

<!-- step -->

## Then: survive first

Before trying to make more money, you will learn how not to lose too much:

- position size;
- stops;
- risk and reward;
- losing streaks;
- fees and leverage; and
- basic account safety.

<!-- step -->

## Then: build a process

Later modules turn the ideas into a written process: entries, exits, a checklist, a journal, testing and a weekly review.

The goal is not to sound confident. It is to make decisions you can explain and review.

<!-- step -->

## What this course does not promise

This course does not provide buy or sell signals. It does not promise a winning strategy, and it does not make the future certain.

It teaches a way to make fewer avoidable mistakes while you learn.

<!-- step -->

## The goal

By the end, you should be able to explain:

- what you expect;
- what would prove you wrong;
- how much you could lose; and
- whether you followed your plan.

**Key idea:** The aim is not perfect prediction. It is a process you can understand, practise and improve.
