# The sums of one trade, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **4 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## The setup

> **Hypothetical.** Account $2,000. Risk 1%. Coin at **$2.00**. The idea is wrong below **$1.90**.

Five sums follow.

<!-- step -->

## Risk, distance, size

1. Money at risk: 1% of $2,000 = **$20**.
2. Distance to the stop: ($2.00 − $1.90) ÷ $2.00 = **5%**.
3. Position size: $20 ÷ 5% = **$400**.

## Target and fees

4. A 2R target is $40, which is 10% on $400: **$2.20**.
5. Fees at 0.1% per side: about **$0.84** round trip, more than 4% of your $20 risk.
