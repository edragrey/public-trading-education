# Six gates, step by step

One gate per screen, then a practice trade. About **4 minutes**.

> **Hypothetical examples.** The trades here are made up to practise the checklist.

<!-- step -->

## Why a checklist?

Pilots run a checklist before every flight, however experienced they are.

Not because they forget how to fly. Because a checklist catches the mistake you'd make **on a bad day**.

<!-- step -->

## The rule

Six yes-or-no questions. **Every one must be yes.**

One no means **no trade**, however good it looks.

<!-- step -->

## Gate 1: Setup

**Does this match my written setup?**

Not "sort of". Not "a better version". If it's not in the plan, it's not a trade.

<!-- step -->

## Gate 2: Stop

**Do I know exactly where I'm wrong?**

A specific price, where the idea fails. Not a round number that feels okay.

<!-- step -->

## Gate 3: Size

**Is my loss at the stop within my risk per trade?**

Position size worked out from the stop, with a little extra for fees. With leverage: does my stop come before liquidation?

<!-- step -->

## Gate 4: Reward

**Is the target realistic, and at least my minimum ratio?**

Somewhere the price has actually turned before, and at least the reward-to-risk my plan asks for.

<!-- step -->

## Gate 5: Conditions

**Is the market okay to trade right now?**

Enough volume, a normal spread, no big announcement about to land.

<!-- step -->

## Gate 6: Me

**Am I okay to trade right now?**

Not chasing a loss. Not reacting to a post I just saw. Within today's loss limit. Not exhausted.

<!-- step -->

## Practice trade

Your plan says: risk **1%**, minimum **2:1**.

- Setup matches. ✓
- Stop at $47.50, entry $50. ✓
- Loss at stop: 1% of account. ✓
- Target $54. That's $4 reward for $2.50 risk: **1.6:1**.
- Market normal, you feel fine. ✓

## "But it would have won!"

Sometimes a trade you skip goes on to win. That's fine.

The checklist isn't trying to catch every winner. It's keeping **every trade inside your rules**. Log the skipped ones, and judge over many trades.

<!-- step -->

## You've got it

**Six gates, all yes, or no trade.**

Write them on a card next to your screen, and answer them out loud before every trade.
