# Liquidity, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## The book is a ladder

Sellers wait at higher prices, buyers at lower ones. A **market buy** takes the cheapest sellers first, then the next.

> **Hypothetical.** 10 coins at $100, 10 at $101, 10 at $103. Buy 25: you pay $100, then $101, then $103.

<!-- step -->

## The price moves

Your average is $101.20, but the last trade is at **$103**. Your order moved the price 3%.

In a deep market, the same order barely moves it.

## Liquidity comes and goes

It is lower at quiet hours, weekends, around big news and in small coins.

That is when slippage is worst.

**Key idea:** prices move when orders eat through the book, and thin books let small orders move a lot.
