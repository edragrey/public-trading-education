# Leverage as a price force, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Forced closes

When leveraged traders run out of margin, the exchange **sells their positions automatically**.

That selling pushes the price lower.

<!-- step -->

## The loop

Lower price liquidates the next group. They sell, the price falls again. This is a **liquidation cascade**.

The same works upward in a **short squeeze**.

<!-- step -->

## You do not need leverage to be affected

Other people's leverage makes the whole market jumpier. Avoid high leverage yourself, and treat funding and open interest as context.

**Key idea:** leverage turns small moves into forced selling or buying, so crowded markets are fragile.
