# Volume and liquidity, step by step

One small idea per screen. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Two words

**Volume:** how much was traded in a period.

**Liquidity:** how easily you can buy or sell without moving the price much.

## Same price, different worlds

Two coins, both priced at $1.

| | Coin A | Coin B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traded in 24 hours | $500 million | $40,000 |
| Spread | 0.01% | 3% |

In coin B, just getting in and out could cost you several percent before the price has moved at all.

## What thin markets do to you

- **Wider spread:** every trade starts with a bigger loss.
- **Bigger slippage:** stops fill at worse prices.
- **Sudden moves:** one large order can push the price a long way.

A 1% risk on paper can become 2% or 3% in reality.

<!-- step -->

## Two checks before any trade

1. **What's the spread?** If it's more than a fraction of a percent, think twice.
2. **Could I get out?** Look at how much is waiting near the price. If your position is a big chunk of it, you'll struggle to sell without moving the price.

**Key idea:** A price is only as reliable as the trading behind it.
