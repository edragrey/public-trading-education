# Volatility and ATR, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## How far does it normally move?

**ATR** is the average size of a candle over a recent period. It tells you how much a price normally moves.

> **Hypothetical.** Price $100, ATR $4: about $4 a day.

<!-- step -->

## Put the stop outside the noise

A $2 stop sits inside a normal $4 day. Noise stops you out.

Try **2 × ATR**: $8 below entry, so **$92**.

<!-- step -->

## Bigger ATR, smaller size

For the same dollar risk, a wider stop means fewer units.

Risk $40, stop distance $8: **5 units**.

**Key idea:** ATR measures how much a price normally moves, so stops are sized to the market.
