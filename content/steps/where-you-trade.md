# Where you trade, step by step

Three words, one picture and two quick checks. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Three words

**Exchange:** the marketplace itself, with the order book.

**Broker:** a company that sends your orders to an exchange. Stock apps are brokers.

**Wallet:** where you hold crypto yourself.

## Who holds the keys?

- **Custodial:** a company holds your coins, like a bank. Easy, but you trust the company.
- **Self-custody:** you hold the **private key**. Nobody can freeze your coins, but if you lose the key, they are gone for good.

> **A rule worth remembering.** If you don't hold the keys, you hold a promise that the company will give you your coins back.

## Centralized or decentralized?

**Centralized (CEX):** a company runs it and holds your funds while you trade.

**Decentralized (DEX):** trading runs through code on a blockchain, straight from your own wallet. No sign-up, but a wrong address, a fake token or a bad approval can cost you everything.

<!-- step -->

## Before you trust a platform

Check its **regulation**, its **fees** and its **security**. Spread your money across places, and never leave more somewhere than you can afford to lose.

**Key idea:** Choose where you trade as carefully as what you trade.
