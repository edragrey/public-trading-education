# A plan for taking profits, step by step

One small idea per screen, with a few quick questions. About **3 minutes**.

<!-- step -->

## Decide while calm

When you are up, emotion decides badly. So write the plan **before** you enter.

Three questions: where is the first target, how much do I sell there, and what happens to the rest?

<!-- step -->

## An example, not advice

> **Hypothetical.** Buy $100, stop $95 (1R = $5). At $110 (2R) sell **half** and move the stop on the rest to $100.

If it falls back, half the gain is banked and the rest cannot lose. If it runs, you still own half.
