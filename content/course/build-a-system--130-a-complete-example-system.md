This is the whole module in one page. It's a **made-up example** to show what a finished system looks like. It's not a recommendation and not a promise of results. Your own should fit your time, costs and nerves.

## The system on one page

> **Name:** Slow Swing (hypothetical)

**1. Market and style**
- One large, liquid market.
- Swing trading on the daily chart. Check once a day, after the daily candle closes.

**2. Context (when I even look)**
- Price above its 50-day average, and recent structure is higher highs and higher lows.

**3. Entry**
- Trigger: a pullback holds above the last swing low, then a daily candle closes back up.
- Order: buy limit just above that candle's high. If it doesn't fill within 3 days, cancel.

**4. Stop**
- Just below the last swing low. Placed immediately after the fill. Never moved further away.

**5. Position size**
- Risk 1% of the account per trade. Size = amount at risk ÷ distance to the stop.
- No more than 3 trades open at once, and none in the same narrative.

**6. Exits**
- Take half off at 2R. Move the stop to break-even.
- Trail the rest below each new swing low.
- Time exit: if nothing has happened after 15 days, leave.

**7. Rules for bad days**
- Daily loss limit: stop for the day after a 2% loss.
- Weekly loss limit: stop for the week after a 4% loss.
- No trading within a few hours of major scheduled news (central bank, inflation).
- After 3 losses in a row, cut size in half until one win.

**8. Pre-trade checklist**
- Does the trade match context, entry and stop exactly?
- Is the stop in? Is the size right?
- Have I checked the calendar?
- Am I calm, rested and not chasing?

**9. Journal and review**
- Log every trade: setup, plan followed (yes/no), result in R, feeling.
- Weekly review: count rule breaks, then look at results.

**10. When I may change a rule**
- Only after at least 30 trades, never mid-week, and only one change at a time.

## Why this works as a model

- It's **written**, so decisions are made calmly in advance.
- It **limits risk first** (1%, loss limits, stops).
- It has **exits planned before entry**.
- It has a **review loop** so the system can improve for the right reasons.
- It **doesn't promise anything**. It just gives you a fair test.

## How to make your own

1. Copy the headings.
2. Replace each line with something you can actually follow.
3. Test it on past data (see the backtest lesson), then paper trade it.
4. Start small with real money, and review honestly.

## Common mistakes

- Copying someone else's system unchanged.
- Making the page so long you can't follow it.
- Skipping the loss limits because "I won't need them".
- Never reviewing it.
