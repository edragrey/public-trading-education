A one-page reminder of this module. Read the lessons first; use this to refresh your memory.

## The essentials

- **Crises:** correlations jump and liquidity thins (March 2020). Small size and no leverage keep you in the game.
- **Stablecoins** are only as good as what backs them (TerraUSD). High yield means hidden risk.
- **Platform risk is real** (FTX). Hold only what you need on a platform; test withdrawals.
- **Crowds** can move prices far beyond plans (GameStop). Size small, don't chase.
- **Taxes:** depend on your country. Keep records from day one and check current rules.
- **Investing vs trading:** different jobs. A diversified low-cost fund is a sound core; trade with a small slice.
- **DeFi, stablecoins, NFTs:** know where the return comes from and who can fail.

## Questions to keep asking

What backs this? Who can fail? How do I get out? How much can I lose?

## Where to go next

Paper trade your written system, keep the journal, review weekly, and grow size slowly.
