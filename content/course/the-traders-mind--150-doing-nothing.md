Trading apps are designed to make you want to tap something. Prices flicker, notifications buzz, and sitting still feels like falling behind. This lesson makes the case for the most underrated skill in trading: **not trading**.

## One word first

**Patience** here means waiting for trades that match your plan, and being comfortable doing nothing in between, even for days.

## Most of the time, there's no trade

If your plan is specific (see the one-page trade plan lesson), your setup won't appear every hour. For a daily-chart system, a few good setups a month might be normal. On all the other days, the right number of trades is **zero**.

That's not a failure. It's the system working.

## Boredom trades

When nothing is happening, it's tempting to lower the bar: a smaller timeframe, a coin you don't normally trade, a setup that's "close enough". These are **boredom trades**.

> **Hypothetical example.** A made-up month for a made-up beginner.

| | Trades | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trades that matched the plan | 4 | +3.2R |
| Boredom trades | 11 | −5.1R |
| **Total** | 15 | **−1.9R** |

Four good trades made money. Eleven extra trades, taken out of boredom, turned the month negative. And they cost fees each time (see the hidden costs lesson in Survive first).

## Not trading costs nothing

When you're in cash, you can't lose money on a trade. There's no stop to get hit, no slippage, no fees. Missing a move is not a loss (see the FOMO lesson). Every trade, on the other hand, carries real risk and real cost.

## What to do instead

Waiting doesn't mean wasting time:

- **Prepare.** Mark areas of interest on your charts, so you're ready when a setup does appear.
- **Set alerts** at those areas, then close the app.
- **Review** your journal (see the weekly review lesson).
- **Learn** something from this course or elsewhere.
- **Go live your life.** Screen time isn't the same as progress.

## Common mistakes

- Opening trades out of boredom.
- Calling a weak setup 'good enough'.
- Feeling that not trading is wasting time.

<details><summary>1. Your setup hasn't appeared all week. Is something wrong?</summary><p>No. A specific setup won't appear every day. Zero trades can be the right number.</p></details>
