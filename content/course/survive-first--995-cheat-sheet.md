A one-page reminder of this module. Read the lessons first; use this to refresh your memory.

## The essentials

- **Risk about 1% per trade.** Size = amount at risk ÷ distance to stop.
- **Stop = where the idea is wrong.** Set it before entering; never move it further away.
- **Reward-to-risk** and **win rate** work together: expectancy = (win rate × average win) - (loss rate × average loss).
- **Losing streaks are normal.** Recovery isn't symmetrical: -50% needs +100%.
- **Costs:** spread, fees, slippage, funding. They add up fast with frequent trading.
- **Diversify risk, not just names.** Correlated trades are one bet.
- **Leverage** shrinks the room between entry and liquidation. **Perps** add funding and margin rules.
- **Staying safe:** check platforms, keep keys private, never act on unsolicited help.

## Quick formulas

- Position size = (account × risk %) ÷ (entry - stop)
- R = what you risk on the trade; a 2R win = twice the risk
- Gain needed to recover = 1 ÷ (1 - loss) - 1

## Watch out for

- Sizing from confidence instead of from the stop.
- Moving a stop "just this once".
- Counting positions instead of total open risk.
