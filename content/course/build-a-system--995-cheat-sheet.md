A one-page reminder of this module. Read the lessons first; use this to refresh your memory.

## The essentials

- **A system** is written rules for market, style, entry, stop, size, exit and review.
- **Style:** pick one that fits your time, costs and nerves (scalping, day, swing, investing).
- **Entry:** context, trigger, order type, stop. No setup means no trade.
- **Exits:** plan both before entering: stop (wrong) and profit rule (right). Never widen a stop.
- **Managing a trade:** check the stop is in, follow the plan, don't meddle.
- **Checklist** before each trade; **journal** after each trade; **weekly review** for rule breaks.
- **Test first:** backtest with exact rules and costs, then paper trade, then trade tiny.
- **Change rules** rarely: enough trades, one change at a time.

## A one-page plan has

Market and style · context · entry · stop · size (about 1% risk) · exits · loss limits · checklist · review rhythm

- **Selling plan:** decide the first target, how much to sell and what happens to the rest before you enter. A trailing stop only moves in your favour. After a big win: lock in, take some out, cool off.

## Watch out for

- Copying someone's system unchanged.
- Tweaking until the backtest looks good.
- Skipping the journal.
