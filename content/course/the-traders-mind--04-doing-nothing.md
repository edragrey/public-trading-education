Trading apps are designed to make you want to tap something. Prices flicker, notifications buzz, and sitting still feels like falling behind. This lesson makes the case for the most underrated skill in trading: **not trading**.

## One word first

**Patience** here means waiting for trades that match your plan, and being comfortable doing nothing in between, even for days.

## Most of the time, there's no trade

If your plan is specific (Build a system, lesson 2), your setup won't appear every hour. For a daily-chart system, a few good setups a month might be normal. On all the other days, the right number of trades is **zero**.

That's not a failure. It's the system working.

## Boredom trades

When nothing is happening, it's tempting to lower the bar: a smaller timeframe, a coin you don't normally trade, a setup that's "close enough". These are **boredom trades**.

> **Hypothetical example.** A made-up month for a made-up beginner.

| | Trades | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trades that matched the plan | 4 | +3.2R |
| Boredom trades | 11 | −5.1R |
| **Total** | 15 | **−1.9R** |

Four good trades made money. Eleven extra trades, taken out of boredom, turned the month negative. And they cost fees each time (Survive first, lesson 8).

## Not trading costs nothing

When you're in cash, you can't lose money on a trade. There's no stop to get hit, no slippage, no fees. Missing a move is not a loss (lesson 1). Every trade, on the other hand, carries real risk and real cost.

## What to do instead

Waiting doesn't mean wasting time:

- **Prepare.** Mark areas of interest on your charts, so you're ready when a setup does appear.
- **Set alerts** at those areas, then close the app.
- **Review** your journal (Build a system, lesson 5).
- **Learn** something from this course or elsewhere.
- **Go live your life.** Screen time isn't the same as progress.

## Try it (no real money)

Look back at your journal (or your paper trades). Mark each trade "plan" or "boredom". Add up the R for each group.

**Key idea:** Most of good trading is waiting. Doing nothing costs nothing; boredom trades cost a lot.
