## The essentials

- **Trend:** higher highs and higher lows (up), lower highs and lower lows (down), or a range. Name the timeframe.
- **Support and resistance** are areas to watch, not promises.
- **Moving average:** the smoothed average of past closes. It lags; it's weak in ranges.
- **RSI:** a 0 to 100 momentum gauge. High or low is context, not a command.
- **Chart patterns** are easy to see afterwards. Use them to place a stop.
- **Market cap vs FDV and unlocks:** more supply needs more buyers.
- **Crypto project check:** what it does, who is behind it, who holds supply, real use, how it makes money, code review.
- **Company check:** revenue, profit, margin, debt, P/E, and results versus expectations.
- **Macro:** inflation, interest rates and the dollar move many markets. Surprises matter, not the numbers.
- **Sentiment:** fear and greed swing; use as context.

- **Trendline and channel:** two swing points make a line, a third touch makes it believable. **Volume:** compare to recent bars; breakouts on thin volume can fail. **ATR:** how far a price normally moves; stop = a multiple of ATR, smaller size when ATR is big. **Top-down:** trend, areas, extras, timing, plan.

- **MACD:** fast average minus slow average; shrinking histogram = fading momentum; lags and whipsaws in ranges. **Bollinger bands:** a stretchy envelope; a squeeze warns of a bigger move, direction unknown. **Fibonacci:** 38.2 / 50 / 61.8% pullback areas, high − (% × move); an area to watch, never a guarantee.

## Watch out for

- Seeing patterns because you want a trade.
- Trading on a single indicator.
- Holding through scheduled big releases without a plan.
- Treating hype as evidence.
