Everyone talks about handling losses. A big **win** needs a plan too. It tempts you to take bigger risks, feel certain, and give it all back.

## Two words first

A **windfall** is a gain much larger than normal for you: a trade that goes far further than planned, or a market that suddenly rockets.

**Withdrawing** means moving some money out of your trading account into somewhere safe, so it is no longer at risk.

## Why a big win is risky

- You feel **brilliant**, so you size up.
- The gain feels like "the market's money", so you gamble with it.
- You feel you **cannot sell**, because the number might keep growing.
- You trade more, and worse, while excited.

None of these is a character flaw. They are normal reactions to a big win, which is why a written rule helps.

## A rule you can write down

> **Hypothetical example.** Your account is $5,000 and one trade pushes it to $8,000, a $3,000 gain. Your rule says:
> 1. **Lock in:** sell part of the position or move the stop up so most of the gain is protected.
> 2. **Take some out:** withdraw a share, for example a third ($1,000), to a separate account you do not trade from.
> 3. **Cool off:** no new trades for a set time, say 24 to 48 hours.
> 4. **Reset size:** go back to your normal risk percent of the account, not a bigger one.

The numbers are made up and the exact shares are yours to choose. What matters is that you decided **before** the windfall.

## Why take money out?

Money in your trading account is at risk. Money you moved out is not. A withdrawal turns a good result into something real, and it stops a hot streak from becoming a bigger loss.

## What not to do

- Don't raise your risk percent because you are "up".
- Don't open lots of new trades to "use" the gain.
- Don't skip the cool-off, because that is exactly when you feel least like pausing.

## Common mistakes

- Having no rule for wins at all.
- Treating profits as free money to gamble with.
- Holding a huge unrealised gain with no stop, because it "can't go wrong".

## Try it (no real money)

Write your own windfall rule: what counts as a big win for you, and what you do in the first 48 hours. Keep it with your trade plan.

**Key idea:** decide in advance how to protect a big gain, because that is the moment you most want to take more risk.
