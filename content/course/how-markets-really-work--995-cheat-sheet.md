## The essentials

- **Supply and demand:** more demand or less supply pushes price up; the reverse pushes it down. Price is not the same as value.
- **Liquidity:** orders eat through the order book. Thin books mean big moves and slippage. Use limit orders in thin markets.
- **Other side:** retail, whales, market makers (earn the spread), funds, exchanges. Avoid fights where you are at a disadvantage.
- **Leverage:** liquidations feed on themselves. Watch open interest, funding and liquidations as signs of crowding, not predictions.
- **Money flows:** inflow minus outflow. ETF flows are measured; "whales are buying" is often a guess. Flows lag.
- **Cycles:** accumulation, markup, distribution, markdown. Shape, not schedule.
- **Rates and money:** higher rates and tighter money tend to weigh on risky assets. Surprises against expectations matter.
- **Inflation, dollar, risk-on / risk-off:** global mood can move crypto with stocks, and the link changes.

- **Global events:** shocks hit through risk-off, commodities, inflation, currencies and regulation. The first reaction often overshoots. Don't trade the first headline.

## Watch out for

- Treating flow, funding or cycle data as a prediction.
- Trading thin coins that a few holders dominate.
- Trading a big data release without a plan.
