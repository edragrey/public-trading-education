## The essentials

- **A market** is a meeting place for buyers and sellers. **Spot** means you own the thing; **derivatives** follow its price and are riskier.
- **Exchange** = the marketplace. **Broker** = sends your orders to it. **Wallet** = holds crypto. No keys, no coins: you hold a promise.
- **Price** = the last deal. **Bid** = best buyer. **Ask** = best seller. **Spread** = the gap, and it's a cost.
- **Order types:** market (now, takes the ask or bid), limit (your price or better, may not fill), stop (a trigger that becomes an order).
- **Long** gains when the price rises, **short** when it falls. A short can lose more than you put in.
- **A candle** shows open, high, low, close for one period. A **timeframe** is how long each candle covers.
- **Volume** and **liquidity** tell you how easily you can trade without moving the price.

## Habits to keep

1. Check where you trade: regulation, fees, security.
2. Prefer a limit order in thin markets.
3. Place the stop as soon as the entry fills.
4. Plan the trade on five lines before you click: asset, reason, entry, stop, target.

5. Others in the market: funds, bots and market makers; size and speed are theirs, patience and a plan are yours.
6. Futures are obligations at a set price; options are rights you pay a premium for. Buying an option risks the premium; selling can lose far more.

## Watch out for

- Chasing with a market order in a fast move.
- Shorting without a stop.
- Leaving large sums on one platform.
