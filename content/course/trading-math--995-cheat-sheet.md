## The essentials

- **Percent change** = (new − old) ÷ old × 100. Always divide by where you started.
- **Recovering a loss:** lose 20% and you need +25%. Lose 50% and you need +100%.
- **+10% then −10%** is not flat. It leaves you down 1%.
- **R** = what you risked. Results in R let you compare trades of any size.
- **Expectancy in R** = average of all your trades, in R. Positive means ahead.
- **Compounding** = gains on gains. Losses compound too, and big swings drag the result down.
- **Position size** = money at risk ÷ distance to the stop (in %).
- **Break-even price** = entry plus enough to cover fees both ways.
- **Linear chart** = equal dollars. **Log chart** = equal percent. Use log for long histories.
- **Sample size:** 10 trades prove almost nothing. Aim for 50 to 100 honest trades with the same rules.

## Quick sums

| Task | Sum |
| --- | --- |
| Money at risk | Account × risk % |
| Position size | Money at risk ÷ stop distance % |
| Target for 2R | Entry + 2 × (entry − stop) |
| Fees per trade | Size × fee % × 2 |
| Needed to recover | 1 ÷ (1 − loss) − 1 |

## Before you trade

- [ ] Do I know the money I could lose, in dollars?
- [ ] Is my size based on my stop, not on a feeling?
- [ ] Have I counted fees?
- [ ] Am I judging results on enough trades?
