Welcome to the last module. You've learned to protect your money, read a market and build a system. This module is about the part that decides whether you actually use any of it: **you**. We start with the most common feeling in trading.

## One word first

**FOMO** (fear of missing out) is the anxious urge to jump into something because it's moving and other people seem to be making money without you.

## What it feels like

You've been watching a coin. You didn't buy. It's up 20% today, then 35%. Your feed is full of people celebrating. Something in your chest says: **now, before it's too late**.

That feeling is normal. Almost every trader knows it. The problem is what it pushes you to do.

## What FOMO makes people do

- **Buy after a big move**, often near a short-term high, because that's when the feeling is strongest.
- **Skip the plan.** No setup, no clear stop, just "get in".
- **Trade too big**, to "make up for" the gains already missed.
- **Pick a stop that's far too wide** (or none), because the price is moving so fast.

Each of those breaks a rule from the first three modules.

> **Hypothetical example.** A made-up coin, a made-up beginner.

The coin runs from $1.00 to $1.60 in two days. The beginner buys at $1.55 with no stop. Two days later it's back at $1.20. That's a −23% loss on a trade that never had a plan, made because of a feeling.

## Why it's so strong

Seeing a gain you didn't get can feel like a **loss**, even though you didn't lose anything. Social media makes it worse: you see the winners posting, not the people who bought late or lost. And price moves fast, which makes waiting feel risky, even though *not trading* costs nothing.

## Three habits that help

**1. Name it.** When you feel the urge, say "this is FOMO". Naming a feeling makes it easier to watch instead of obey.

**2. Run the checklist anyway** (Build a system, lesson 3). If it's a real trade, it'll pass all six gates. If you can't place a stop or the reward-to-risk is poor because the price already ran, the checklist gives you a calm "no".

**3. Remember there's always another trade.** Markets open every day. Missing one move costs you nothing. Chasing it can cost a lot.

## Try it (no real money)

For the next week, every time you feel the urge to jump into something because it's moving, write down: what, the price, and "FOMO". A week later, check those prices. Notice how many you're glad you didn't chase.

**Key idea:** FOMO is a feeling, not a signal. Name it, run your checklist, and remember that missing a move costs nothing.
