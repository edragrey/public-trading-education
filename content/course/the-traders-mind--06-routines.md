Most of this course is rules. Rules work best when they're wrapped in a **routine**: the same small steps, in the same order, every time. Routines take decisions off your plate, so your energy goes where it matters.

## One word first

**Routine** here means a short, repeatable set of steps you do before, during and after trading.

## Before you trade (10 minutes)

1. **How am I?** Rested, calm, not rushed? If not, today might be a no-trading day. That's allowed.
2. **What's on the calendar?** Any big announcements today (Read the market, lesson 9)?
3. **Where are my areas?** Look at the charts you follow and note where your setup could appear.
4. **Read your plan.** Yes, every time. One page, one minute.
5. **Know today's limit** (lesson 5): what's the most you can lose today?

## While you trade

- **Six gates before every trade** (Build a system, lesson 3).
- **Journal the "before" part** before you click (Build a system, lesson 4).
- **Set the stop straight away**, as part of opening the trade.
- **Then step back.** Staring at an open trade rarely helps, and often leads to moving stops.

## After you trade (5 minutes)

1. **Fill in the "after"** part of the journal.
2. **One line on how you felt.** Calm? Rushed? Bored?
3. **Close the app.**

## The basics that quietly matter

Nobody likes this part, but it's true: **sleep, food, stress and alcohol** all affect decisions. Tired traders take worse trades. If you wouldn't drive in a certain state, think twice before trading in it.

## Keep it small enough to keep

A routine you'll actually do is better than a perfect one you won't. Start with three steps and add more only when they're automatic.

> **Hypothetical example.** A minimal routine: "Before: read my plan and check the calendar. During: six gates every time. After: journal and close the app."

<details><summary>2. Why set your stop at the same moment you open the trade?</summary><p>So there's never a gap where you're in a trade with no exit, and so you don't have to decide it later under pressure.</p></details>

## Try it (no real money)

Write a three-step "before", a one-step "during" and a two-step "after". Stick it next to your trade plan and use it for one week.

**Key idea:** Wrap your rules in a small routine you repeat every time. Good habits become automatic, and you have more calm left for the decisions that matter.
