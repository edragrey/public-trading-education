Twelve quick questions on this module. Six are about this module and four bring back earlier ideas, mixed in on purpose: recalling old ideas is what makes them stick. Tap an answer to see why. No score is kept.

## 1. Why write your system down?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">So decisions are made calmly in advance</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">So it looks professional</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">So you never need to review it</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>A written plan beats improvising under pressure.</p>
</div>

## 2. What are the parts of a written entry?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">Only a price</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">Context, trigger, order type and stop</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">A hunch and a size</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>A good entry is specific and includes the stop.</p>
</div>

## 3. Account $10,000, risk 1%, stop 5% away. What position size?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">$500</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">$2,000</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">$5,000</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Risk is $100. $100 / 0.05 = $2,000.</p>
</div>

## 4. Which stop move is acceptable?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">Moving it to reduce risk</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">Moving it further away</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">Removing it in a fast market</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Never widen a stop; reducing risk is fine.</p>
</div>

## 5. Price nears your stop and you feel nervous, but nothing changed. What do you do?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">Move the stop away</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">Follow the plan</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">Add to the position</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Feelings aren't new information about the trade.</p>
</div>

## 6. What does it mean that a moving average lags?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">It predicts the next move</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">It reacts after price has already moved</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">It ignores old prices</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>It's built from past prices, so it confirms moves rather than forecasting them.</p>
</div>

## 7. Why list every trade when backtesting?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">To count results honestly, including the ugly ones</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">To pick the best trades</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">To speed it up</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Hindsight tempts you to skip bad trades.</p>
</div>

## 8. Four highly correlated trades each risk 1%. About how much can one bad day cost?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">1%</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">Close to 4%</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">0.25%</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Correlated trades tend to lose together, so the risks add up.</p>
</div>

## 9. When may you change a rule?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">After enough trades, one change at a time</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">After any loss</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">Mid-trade</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Frequent tweaks make results impossible to read.</p>
</div>

**Key idea:** Wrong answers are useful. Go back to the lesson behind any question you missed.

## 10. Your stop is hit at a worse price than you set. What's that called?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">Slippage</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">Spread</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">Funding</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Slippage is the gap between the price you expected and the price you got.</p>
</div>

## 11. When should you decide how you will take profit?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">Before you enter</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">When the trade is up</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">After it reverses</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Decide while calm. Once you are up, emotion pushes you to sell too early or hold too long.</p>
</div>

## 12. Which way does a trailing stop move?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">Only in your favour</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">Up and down with price</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">Only down</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>A trailing stop follows price in your favour and never moves back.</p>
</div>
