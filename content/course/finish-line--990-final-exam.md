Twenty questions from every part of the course. They're mixed on purpose: recalling things you learned weeks ago is what makes them stick. Tap an answer to see whether it's right and why. No score is kept, and you can take it as often as you like. This is a self-check, not an accredited exam.

## 1. What is a stop-loss for?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="c">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">Guaranteeing profit</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">Making the trade bigger</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">Limiting how much one trade can lose</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>A stop is a pre-decided exit that caps the loss. It never guarantees profit, and it doesn't increase size.</p>
</div>

## 2. You risk $50 to make $150. Roughly what win rate breaks even, before costs?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">75%</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">25%</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">50%</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Risk 1 to win 3: you need to win about 1 in 4 trades to break even.</p>
</div>

## 3. Account $2,000, risk 1% per trade, stop $4 below entry. How many units?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="c">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">500</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">50</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">5</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>1% of $2,000 is $20 at risk. $20 ÷ $4 per unit = 5 units.</p>
</div>

## 4. A coin falls 50%. What gain brings it back?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">100%</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">50%</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">25%</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>From $100 to $50 you need to double ($50 to $100), a 100% gain.</p>
</div>

## 5. What does a higher high with a higher low suggest?

<button type="button" data-opt="b">A range</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">A downtrend</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Higher highs and higher lows are the pattern of an uptrend.</p>
</div>

## 6. An RSI above 70 means the price must fall soon.

## 11. Why do liquidations pile up in a fall when many traders use leverage?

## 12. A central bank raises interest rates. What is the usual pressure on risky assets?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
