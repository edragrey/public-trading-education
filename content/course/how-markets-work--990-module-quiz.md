Twelve quick questions on this module. Tap an answer to see whether it's right and why. No score is kept, and you can read the lesson again any time.

## 1. What does 'spot' trading mean?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">You own the actual share or coin</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">You hold a contract that only follows the price</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">You borrow it from a broker for a fee</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Spot means you own the thing. A contract that follows the price is a derivative.</p>
</div>

## 2. The best bid is $50.00 and the best ask is $50.10. You place a market buy. About what do you pay?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">$50.00, the bid</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">$50.10, the ask</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">$50.05, the middle</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>A market buy takes the best available seller, so you pay the ask.</p>
</div>

## 3. What is the spread?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="c">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">The difference between the day's high and low</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">The fee your broker charges per month</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">The gap between the best bid and the best ask</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>The spread is a small cost you pay each time you trade.</p>
</div>

## 4. Which order only fills at your price or better, and may never fill?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">A limit order</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">A market order</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">A stop-market order</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>A limit order waits for your price. Market orders fill now.</p>
</div>

## 5. If you don't hold the keys to your crypto, what do you hold?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">Full ownership, same as holding the keys</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">A promise from the company to give it back</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">Insurance against losing it</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Without the keys, you rely on the platform to return your coins.</p>
</div>

## 6. Why can a price move with no news?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="c">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">Prices only move on news</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">The exchange sets the price each morning</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">Buyers or sellers were simply more eager, and used up the orders waiting</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Every move is deals between buyers and sellers. News is only one reason for eagerness.</p>
</div>

## 7. What does one candlestick show?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">The open, high, low and close for one time period</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">Only the closing price</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">The number of trades made</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>A candle summarises four prices for its period.</p>
</div>

## 8. Going short means you gain when…

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">The price rises</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">The price falls</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">The market is closed</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>A short is a bet on a fall. Its losses can be larger than the amount put in.</p>
</div>

## 9. Why can a short lose more than the money put in?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="c">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">Exchanges charge a penalty</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">Shorts can never use stops</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">A price can rise without limit</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>A long can fall to zero. A short has no ceiling.</p>
</div>

## 10. What's the main point of a paper trade?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">Practise the plan, sizing and review without real money</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">Prove a strategy works</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">Win as much pretend money as possible</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Paper trading builds process and habits. It doesn't prove an edge.</p>
</div>

## 11. What does a market maker earn?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="b">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">A commission from the exchange on every trader</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">The small gap between its buy and sell prices</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">A share of every trader's profit</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>Market makers quote both a buy and a sell price and keep the spread between them.</p>
</div>

## 12. What is the most you can lose when you buy an option?

<div class="quiz" data-answer="a">
<button type="button" data-opt="a">The premium you paid</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="b">Unlimited amounts</button>
<button type="button" data-opt="c">Ten times the premium</button>
<p class="quiz-why" hidden>An option buyer has a right, not an obligation, so the loss stops at the premium. Sellers are a different story.</p>
</div>
