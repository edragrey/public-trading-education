Sooner or later you'll lose five, six, maybe ten trades in a row. It feels personal. It usually isn't. This lesson shows why streaks are normal, and how to make sure one doesn't end your trading.

## Two words first

**Losing streak** is a run of losing trades one after another.

**Drawdown** is how far your account has fallen from its last high point. If you were at $2,000 and you're now at $1,700, that's a 15% drawdown.

## Streaks are normal, even for good strategies

> **Hypothetical example.** These are rough odds from simple probability, not a forecast for any real strategy.

If you win **50%** of trades, then over **100 trades** a run of about **5 or 6 losses in a row** is likely to show up somewhere. If you win **40%** of trades, runs of **7 or 8** are not unusual.

That's not because the strategy broke. It's what randomness looks like. Coins land tails six times in a row more often than people think.

## How deep does the hole get?

It depends almost entirely on how much you risk per trade (the 1% lesson).

| Losses in a row | At 1% risk | At 2% risk | At 5% risk | At 10% risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | −5% | −10% | −23% | −41% |
| 10 | −10% | −18% | −40% | −65% |
| 15 | −14% | −26% | −54% | −79% |

These use the same percentage of whatever is left after each loss, so the numbers are a bit smaller than simple multiplication.

## Planning for it in advance

The time to decide what you'll do in a drawdown is **before** it happens, while you're calm. A few common rules people write down:

- **Cut size in a drawdown.** For example: "If I'm down 10% from my high, I halve my risk per trade until I'm back."
- **Pause after a streak.** For example: "After 5 losses in a row, I stop for the day and review."
- **A hard floor.** For example: "If I'm down 20%, I stop trading real money for a month and go back to paper."

The exact numbers are yours to choose. Having them written down is what matters.

## What a streak can tell you

Usually a streak is just noise. But it's worth one honest check: **did I follow my plan on these trades?** If you did, it's probably normal variance. If you didn't (moved stops, traded bigger, skipped your checklist), the problem isn't the strategy. The Trader's mind module covers this.

## Common mistakes

- Raising risk after a loss to catch up.
- Taking a normal streak as proof the system is broken.
- Having no written plan for a drawdown.
