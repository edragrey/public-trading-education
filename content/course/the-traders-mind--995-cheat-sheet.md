## Understand yourself

- **The market gives no rules.** Structure comes from you.
- **Think in probabilities:** an edge shows over many trades, not one.
- **Five truths:** anything can happen; you needn't predict to profit; wins and losses arrive randomly; an edge is only a tilt; every moment is unique.
- **Accept the risk first:** know the loss at your stop and be okay with it.
- **Four fears:** being wrong, losing money, missing out, leaving money behind. Name them, shrink size, return to the plan.
- **Beliefs:** notice, question, replace, then practise in small size.
- **Consistency:** same risk, checklist, exits and journal. Review in batches.
- **Responsibility:** own your decisions; set process goals.

## Protect yourself

- **FOMO:** chasing after a move. If you missed it, you lost nothing.
- **Revenge and tilt:** trading to win it back. Stop, step away, review.
- **Overconfidence:** winning streaks can be luck. Keep size steady.
- **Doing nothing** is a position. Skip days with no setup.
- **Loss limits:** daily and weekly. Decide them while calm.
- **Routines:** same prep, same checklist, same review.
- **Big loss:** stop, write what happened, cut size, return slowly, talk to someone.
- **Information diet:** few reliable sources, check on a schedule, treat unverified claims as rumours.
- **Process over outcome:** score the decision and the result separately.

## Quick pre-trade statement

"I'm risking $__. I accept I might lose it. If the stop is hit, I take it and move on."

## Quick self-check before a trade

Am I calm? Is this my setup? Is the stop in and the size right? Am I chasing?

- **Selling is hard:** decide exits in advance and judge the plan, not the outcome. **Coming back after a big loss:** paper first, very small size, climb a ladder on rule-following trades.

## Watch out for

- Growing size after wins or losses.
- Refreshing feeds mid-trade.
- Judging yourself by one result.
